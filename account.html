Frequently Asked Questions

How are Chronova timepieces inspected before dispatch?
Every single Chronova watch undergoes 847 rigorous quality checks in our atelier, including 5-position chronometric timing, water pressure testing, and hand-polishing inspection before release.
What is included in the 5-Year Atelier Warranty?
The Chronova International Warranty covers mechanical movement defects, crystal integrity, and complimentary ultrasonic cleaning and bracelet recalibration at any authorized atelier.
What is the return and exchange policy?
We offer a seamless 14-day vault return policy for unworn timepieces in their original velvet packaging with seals intact. Complimentary courier pickup is arranged.
How do I resize my Jubilee or President link bracelet?
Each solid bracelet comes with a precision micro-pin adjustment tool. You may also visit any certified jeweler or contact our concierge for customized pre-dispatch sizing.
