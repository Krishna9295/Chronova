Geneva Ateliers Since 1985

Four Decades of Haute Horlogerie

A heritage defined by tireless mechanical obsession, hand-finished movements, and enduring Swiss perfection crafted for those who command their destiny.

1985
The Foundation in Geneva
Founded on the shores of Lake Geneva by master horologists seeking to unify centuries-old Swiss balance-wheel traditions with ultra-modern metallurgy.
2001
The Tourbillon Calibre I
Introduction of Chronova's signature patented flying tourbillon, compensating for gravity with flawless aesthetic symmetry and 28,800 VpH precision.
2014
Ceramic & Damascus Steel
Pioneered custom high-tech forged carbon cases and 316L surgical steel alloys resistant to extreme nautical and aviation pressures.
Present
The Eternal Legacy
Continuing to craft limited-edition mechanical masterworks with bespoke bespoke atelier services for the world's most discerning watch collectors.
